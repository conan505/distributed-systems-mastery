# Behavioral and Leadership Questions

## Common Behavioral Questions

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
- Tell me about a time when you took complete ownership of a project.
- How do you prioritize work when everything is urgent?
- Describe a situation where you failed. What did you learn?
- Give an example of when you made a decision with incomplete data.
- Tell me about a time when you mentored someone.
- How do you deal with ambiguity?
- Describe a time you had to push back on a stakeholder.
- When have you gone above and beyond your job responsibilities?
- Tell me about a time when you had to resolve a conflict within your team.
- Have you ever made a tough trade-off between quality and speed?
- Tell me about a time you influenced a decision without authority.
- How do you handle negative feedback?
- Tell me about your biggest mistake and how you handled it.
- Walk me through a high-pressure situation and how you handled it.
- How do you manage expectations when timelines slip?
- Tell me about a time when you simplified a complex problem.
- Describe a time you improved a process or tool.
- Share an example where your idea was rejected — what did you do?
- Describe a time you shipped something impactful under tight deadlines.
- How do you make sure your team stays aligned on goals?
- Tell me about a time you helped someone succeed.
- What does leadership mean to you in a tech team?

## Miscellaneous Interview Questions

*Asked at companies like Razorpay, Gojek, Atlassian, and Paytm:*

- Design a system to notify users in real-time when their transaction status changes.
- How would you implement event-driven architecture in a ticket booking app?
- What if the webhook fails? How do you ensure delivery reliability?
- Compare long polling, short polling, and webhooks — when to use what?
- How do you secure webhook endpoints?
- If a webhook is delayed or lost, how will you handle data consistency?
